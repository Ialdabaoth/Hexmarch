# Hexmarch Scribe: changelog

The Hexmarch Scribe app (`scribe/`, installed as "⬢ Scribe"), the module and campaign editor. The released version lives in `VERSION`; every merged PR that touches Scribe is one patch, each planned sprint bumps the minor. New entries are not written here by hand: each PR adds a change note in `changes/`, and the site build numbers the notes in the order they merged and lists them here (CONTRIBUTING.md "Versions and change notes"). The spec is `modules/SCRIBE.md` in the data repo.

## 0.6.x (in development)

Sprint (roadmap step 3): integrate real tools, only ones free to integrate, starting with the fundamentals of remote play; finish the campaign view (steps 4 to 6) and the update checker; rename the protocol names to `hexmarch-`.

- 0.6.0: Scribe jumps from 0.1.x to 0.6.0 so that all three apps share one minor version (Heph 2026-11-09, after the alpha walkthrough worked end to end: create a campaign, publish, invite, accept, roll, send, approve, explore the map as a character). No behaviour change. 0.1.x in short: the shared editing engine; the campaign view's steps 1 to 3 (name, rules, modules, start), 7 (save on this device and open in the Companion or the Atlas, with the creator's Referee invitation), 8 (where players find it, with the Test button) and 9 (Publish and Publish again); the apps hand each other the campaign and play by links; the app is named Hexmarch Scribe. Not built in 0.1.x and carried into 0.6.x: steps 4 to 6 (settle conflicts and slice modules, own content, parties and secrets) and the update checker.

## 0.1.x

Sprint: the campaign view (pick a root, add modules, pick a start, settle conflicts, own content, parties and secrets, open in the apps) and the update checker. Steps 4 to 6 and the update checker moved to 0.6.x.

- 0.1.10: The app is now "Hexmarch Scribe", not "E9 Scribe" (Heph 2026-11-09: the apps run any OSR hexcrawl core rules); the name comes from site.json "name".
- 0.1.9: A saved draft whose modules aren't on the data site (offline, or the site moved) no longer stops the page.
- 0.1.8: Apps talk by links: "Save and open in the Companion" hands the campaign over in a `#m=` link (with the state to start from when Scribe just wrote it), and Scribe takes play the Companion sends back by link ("Update in Scribe") for Publish.
- 0.1.7: A **Test** button beside "Folder you upload to" (Heph 2026-11-09). It fetches `campaign.json` and `snapshot.json` from the folder, past any cache, and compares each with what the last publish produced: it matches, it isn't there yet (a new upload can take a minute or two to appear), it is an older one, it is a different file, or it can't be read (not JSON, or the host doesn't let other sites read it). Publishing keeps that pair on the device as `published:<id>` for the test; the check itself is `checkUpload` in the shared engine.
- 0.1.6: Publish again keeps what play has added. Scribe now builds the snapshot from the same parts as the Companion's Update campaign (`publishParts` in the shared engine): the state as it stands now, each party member's knowledge for their player alone, and the join requests the referee has answered as receipts. Before, publishing again from Scribe after players joined left the knowledge and receipts out until the next Update campaign.
- 0.1.5: Steps 8 and 9 of the campaign view, Services and Publish (SCRIBE.md section 5; WORKFLOWS.md "Invitations"). Once a campaign is saved on this device, **Where players find it** takes a folder on any free web host the referee can upload to (and, optionally, where players send requests), and **Publish** writes the two files players read: `campaign.json`, the campaign pointer that invitations name, and `snapshot.json`, the campaign, its modules and the shared state as they stand on this device now (nothing referee-only). Scribe hands both over to upload into the folder and records the campaign as published, which is what lets the Companion offer Invite a player. Publishing again counts the revision on. The pointer and snapshot are built by the shared engine (`engine/publish.ts`), so the Companion's Update campaign writes the same files.
- 0.1.4: A ready campaign gets its creator's Referee invitation (SCRIBE.md step 7 "Ready", Heph 2026-11-09). Saving a campaign on this device (any of the three save buttons) also adds a Referee invitation for it to the Companion's invitations on this device, unless one is already there, so the Companion's campaign switcher lists it and opens it in Referee mode. Every other invitation is made in the Companion.
- 0.1.3: step 7, open the campaign. **Save and open in the Companion** and **Save and open the start in the Atlas** save the campaign on this device (the apps share one site, so they share its storage: the campaign module and lockfile as `scribe-campaign:<id>`, the first state file as `state:<id>`) and open it there, with no files to move by hand. The Companion makes it the campaign in use (Companion 0.5.15). The Atlas opens on the start's place and puts the party there (Atlas 0.5.16). **Save on this device** only saves. If this device already has play for the campaign, Scribe asks before starting it again from the start; otherwise it keeps the play and saves only the campaign. The three downloads stay.
- 0.1.2: the campaign view, steps 1 to 3. Name the campaign, pick its rules (each rules module and each of its options is a root: E9, Materia Mundi, Materia Mundi for E9), pick modules from the data site (or `?site=` / "Modules from another site"), and pick one of the modules' starts or place the party yourself (module, place, date, hour). Scribe resolves the modules with the engine and explains anything that blocks them. It writes three files to download: the campaign `module.json` (`kind: "campaign"`, the root first in `requires`, the root's `option`, the start, and `exclude` for the parts a start leaves out), `campaign.lock.json` (`e9-lock/1`) and the first `state.json` (`e9-campaign-state/1`: clock from the start, the start's places revealed, no party yet). "Open the start in the Atlas" opens the start's place. The draft is kept on this device. Scribe is now a Vite + Svelte app (`scribe/src/`, its own `package.json`); `scribe_app.py` copies its build and adds the install manifest and icons. Needs Atlas 0.5.13's manifests (`semver`, `starts`) to offer a module's starts.
- 0.1.1: the shared editing engine (`engine/`, see its README). Resolves a module's dependencies to one version each and writes the lockfile, refuses two roots and declared conflicts, stitches the modules' content with overrides, the referee's decisions, exclusions and field patches, and reports patches whose field the author has since changed. Tests run with the Companion's. Scribe's page does not use it yet.
- 0.1.0: Scribe exists at `<site>/scribe/` as its own installable app: a placeholder page, its own install manifest (`scribe/manifest.webmanifest`: name "⬢ Scribe", short name "Scribe", start and scope its own folder) and its icon (`icons/scribe.svg`). Built by `scribe/scribe_app.py` in the site workflow, after the Atlas.
