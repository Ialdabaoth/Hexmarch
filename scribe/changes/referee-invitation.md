A ready campaign gets its creator's Referee invitation (SCRIBE.md step 7 "Ready", Heph 2026-11-09). Saving a campaign on this device (any of the three save buttons) also adds a Referee invitation for it to the Companion's invitations on this device, unless one is already there, so the Companion's campaign switcher lists it and opens it in Referee mode. Every other invitation is made in the Companion.
