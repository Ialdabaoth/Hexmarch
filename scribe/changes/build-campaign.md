**Build Campaign** (Heph 2026-11-10) replaces "Save on this device" and the two "Save and open…" buttons. Building saves the campaign and your Referee invitation on this device. Only then do Campaign Files, **Preview My Campaign** (Preview in Companion, Preview in Atlas), Where players find it and Publish appear. Changing the campaign after building hides them again until you build it again. The "Play it" heading is gone; its tooltip is on "Your campaign".
