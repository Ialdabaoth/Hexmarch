Steps 8 and 9 of the campaign view, Services and Publish (SCRIBE.md section 5; WORKFLOWS.md "Invitations"). Once a campaign is saved on this device, **Where players find it** takes a folder on any free web host the referee can upload to (and, optionally, where players send requests), and **Publish** writes the two files players read: `campaign.json`, the campaign pointer that invitations name, and `snapshot.json`, the campaign, its modules and the shared state as they stand on this device now (nothing referee-only). Scribe hands both over to upload into the folder and records the campaign as published, which is what lets the Companion offer Invite a player. Publishing again counts the revision on. The pointer and snapshot are built by the shared engine (`engine/publish.ts`), so the Companion's Update campaign writes the same files.
