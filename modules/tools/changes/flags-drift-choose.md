Footing and the lost roll, redesigned (Heph 2026-11-16). The runner and the Atlas now carry what E9's new rules need:
- Party flags: a `flag` effect sets one (such as `on-course`) until the next shift, kept on the party; procedures read `{flags.<name>}`, a roll's `"easier": "flags.on-course"` makes it a step easier, and a branch can test a flag.
- Drift: a lost party (`drift` effect) keeps walking, each hex turned one hex-side off its way, until a passing lost check ends it; the walk then heads for the route's next point. The Events box shows the drift and the flags to the referee.
- Choose: per member who failed, the referee picks an option in the Events log (stuck or injured); several members stuck cost the party one hour in all.
- Clock triggers take the ruleset's own time units (E9 "shift"), and triggers due at the same boundary run in list order.
