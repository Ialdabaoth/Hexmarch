Rolls along the way (E9, Heph 2026-11-15). The procedures runner can roll for every party member in a mode: an `each` step names the mode (or `*` for everyone), the ruleset check to roll and the grade it rolls against, and combines the results as `any` (one success is enough), `all` (everyone must succeed) or `each` (one outcome per member). The answer branches like any roll, and an effect with `for` (`"for": "r.failed"`) is applied to each member it lists, such as 1d6 damage to each marcher who failed. A `secret` roll is marked as such in the log. A walk step's `then` procedures run in every hex after the walked one, with the same params, and are left out of previews and estimates, so the walked `move` stays roll-free; a `halt` in them stops the walk in that hex. The party box records Help: a member on a mode that grants help picks an ally and either one more mode for them or one of their modes made easier, kept per party (`parties[].help`); each Help eases that mode's rolls a step, never past easy. The Events log shows who rolled what, against which grade, and whether it passed.
