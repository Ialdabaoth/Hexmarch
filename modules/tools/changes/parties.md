Parties (Heph 2026-11-10). A campaign state holds any number of parties (`parties[]`, `active`; STATE_FORMAT.md "Parties"), each with its own start, clock and session. The party creator is in the referee's party box. It takes a name, one start from the campaign's modules (its lockfile: Scribe's record on the referee's device, the snapshot on a player's, or `{lock}` from the app around the Atlas; every module the site lists when no campaign is loaded) (or "Place the party yourself", with a date and an hour), and the party's clock, which the start sets. A party with a module start is placed at that start's place when its map opens. One party is shown and moved at a time: `?party=<id>`, else the party of the character shown (`?as=`, so a player's locked view follows its character's party), else the active one. The referee switches parties in the box and sees every other party's marker as a small slate pennant with its name. A move passes time on that party's own clock and no longer adds the arrival hex to everyone's revealed set: the members get what they see as knowledge, as before. Messages to the app around the Atlas carry the party: `{event: 'party', party, hex, hours}`, `{event: 'party-new', party}` and `{event: 'active', party}`. The separate party message from the app is gone, because the state carries every party. The `?place=` link and the top-level `clock`, `party` and `session` are gone (clean break). The referee's party box has "Invite a player" for the party shown (Heph 2026-11-10: Scribe sets up the campaign, the Atlas builds the party, and the Atlas hands to the Companion once per invitation). When the app around the Atlas offers it (`{hostActions: ['invite']}`), the button sends `{event: 'invite', module, party}` and the host opens the Companion's invitation for that party. Standalone, it opens the referee's Companion with a hexmarch-mail `campaign` link (`#m=`, carrying Scribe's record, the state and `{invite: {party}}`), and the Companion opens its Invite a player with that party picked. When this device has no Scribe record, the Atlas shows the player's invitation link itself, `<Companion>?campaign=<campaign link>&as=player&party=<id>`, with Copy and "Open in the Companion". The campaign link is the published pointer: the settings' link when it is one, Scribe's or the Companion's record of it, or `{campaign}` from the host. Until the campaign is published the box says to publish it first.
