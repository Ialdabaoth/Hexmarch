Parties (Heph 2026-11-10). A campaign state holds any number of parties (`parties[]`, `active`; STATE_FORMAT.md "Parties"), each with its own start, clock and session. The party creator is in the referee's party box. It takes a name, one start from the campaign's modules (its lockfile: Scribe's record on the referee's device, the snapshot on a player's, or `{lock}` from the app around the Atlas; every module the site lists when no campaign is loaded) (or "Place the party yourself", with a date and an hour), and the party's clock, which the start sets. A party with a module start is placed at that start's place when its map opens. One party is shown and moved at a time: `?party=<id>`, else the party of the character shown (`?as=`, so a player's locked view follows its character's party), else the active one. The referee switches parties in the box and sees every other party's marker as a small slate pennant with its name. A move passes time on that party's own clock and no longer adds the arrival hex to everyone's revealed set: the members get what they see as knowledge, as before. Messages to the app around the Atlas carry the party: `{event: 'party', party, hex, hours}`, `{event: 'party-new', party}` and `{event: 'active', party}`. The separate party message from the app is gone, because the state carries every party. The `?place=` link and the top-level `clock`, `party` and `session` are gone (clean break). A host may offer actions it carries out itself: `{hostActions: ['companion']}` shows an "Open in the Companion" button in the referee view, which sends `{event: 'companion', party}` (Scribe answers with the campaign's link).
