- Footing and the lost roll, redesigned (Heph): each shift, Navigate rolls first. A pass puts the party on course until the next shift, which makes footing and Sneak one step easier (stacks with Help, never past easy). A fail means the party drifts off course but keeps walking. Footing is rolled once a shift instead of in every hex, and never halts: each member who fails chooses stuck (lose an hour) or injured (1d6).
