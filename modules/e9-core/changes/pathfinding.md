- Footing and the lost roll, redesigned (Heph): each shift, Navigate rolls first; a fail means the party drifts off course but keeps walking. A new exploration action, Pathfinding (Survival vs traverse), follows: a pass makes every Agility and Stealth roll that shift one step easier (footing, Forced March, Sneak; stacks with Help, never past easy). Footing is rolled once a shift instead of in every hex, and never halts: each member who fails chooses stuck (lose an hour) or injured (1d6).
