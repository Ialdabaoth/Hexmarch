- Movement (Heph, 2026-11-15) in `rules.json` under `movement` and `time`, explained in `movement.md`. 1x is 5 paces a round, 5 furlongs a turn or 3 miles an hour. The modes are On Watch, Navigate, Sneak, Climb/Swim/Maneuver, Forced March, Dash, Investigate and Help, and Experts get more of them as they level. The slowest mode sets a character's speed, and the slowest member sets the party's.
- Terrain traits replace travel multipliers. `rules.json` `terrain` gives E9's default orient, traverse, hide, investigate and view grades for each Atlas terrain id, and `movement.traverse` says what a traverse grade does (hard forces Climb/Swim/Maneuver, impossible stops travel). `move` divides by the party's speed and rolls nothing, `sight-range` answers by the view grade, and `travel` is retired. Every road is 1x; road quality only eases the Forced March roll (easy on a Roman or local road, normal on a trail).
