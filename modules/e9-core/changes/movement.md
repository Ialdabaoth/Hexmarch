- Movement scales and modes (Heph, 2026-11-15) in `rules.json` under `movement` and `time`, explained in `movement.md`. 1x is 5 paces a round, 5 furlongs a turn or 3 miles an hour. The modes are On Watch, Navigate, Sneak, Climb/Swim/Maneuver, Forced March, Dash, Investigate and Help. Experts get more modes as they level. The slowest mode sets a character's speed, and the slowest member sets the party's. E9's procedures follow it: `travel` answers 2 on difficult terrain, the new `terrain-kind` answers difficult or open, and `move` divides by the party's speed (from the Atlas, #68) without counting difficult ground twice.
