- The shift and Forced March rolls: `lost` (each shift, everyone on Navigate rolls Survival at the hex's orient grade in secret; if nobody passes, the party is lost and the walk halts) and `forced-march` (each hour, every marcher rolls Agility at the road's grade, easy on Roman and local roads and normal elsewhere; each failure takes 1d6 damage). Clock triggers `lost-shift` (every 4 hours) and `forced-march-hour` (every hour on Forced March) run them while walking. Help on Climb/Swim/Maneuver now eases the footing roll.
