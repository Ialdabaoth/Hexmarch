A campaign's Discord channel works as its mailbox and roll log when its "Where players send requests" address is a Discord webhook. A player's join request posts there as a readable line ("⏳ Dana asks to join with Holly") with the request attached as a file, and the player's Companion edits that same post as it moves on ("✅ joined" or "↩️ sent back"). Everyone's rolls go to the campaign's channel with nothing to paste (a webhook saved in Settings still goes first). In referee mode, Campaign › Tell the table posts a description or a handout picture to the channel.
