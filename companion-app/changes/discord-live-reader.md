Referee mode reads the campaign's Discord channel live: in Settings › Discord, the referee saves their own bot's token (kept on this device, per campaign), and while the Companion is open it holds Discord's gateway, hears each new post in the channel the campaign webhook posts to, and puts any join request file posted there straight into the Inbox. Settings shows whether it is listening, and why not (no server, can't see the channel, Message Content Intent off, token refused). Posts made while the app is closed are not picked up yet.
