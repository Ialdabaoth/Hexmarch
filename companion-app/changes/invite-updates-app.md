Tapping an invitation on a device with an old copy of the app no longer ends at "That link isn't a Materia Mundi campaign file". A campaign file in a format the app doesn't know says "This campaign needs a newer Companion. Updating…", fetches the new version and opens the invitation again after reloading. When a new version takes over just after the app opens, or while an invitation waits, the page reloads into it.
