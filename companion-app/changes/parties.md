Parties (STATE_FORMAT, MMRPG #172): a campaign holds a list of parties instead of one party, clock and session. Each party has its own name, start, clock, sessions and place on the map; the referee makes and picks parties in the Atlas (Maps › Party), and the Companion follows. Campaign › State shows the party in use with a switcher, each character can be moved to another party, and an invitation is to a party (`&party=` in the link; a join request carries it, and the approved character joins that party). Player mode follows the character's own party. State files from before parties are not read; re-make test campaigns.
