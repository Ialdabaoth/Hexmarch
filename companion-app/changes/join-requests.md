The alpha round trip works between two devices. A player sends their character to the referee from the home screen: by the campaign's post URL when it has one, otherwise as a file. The referee opens it in the Inbox and approves it or sends it back with a note. Settings › Update campaign then saves the campaign pointer and a new snapshot for upload, carrying the state, each character's knowledge and the answered requests. A player's Check for changes takes the new party list and their own character's knowledge, and the Inbox follows the request from Sent to Received to Approved.
