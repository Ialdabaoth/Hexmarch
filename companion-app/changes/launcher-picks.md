The Companion opens on what the Launcher picked for a campaign (`hexmarch-current`: `{campaign, seat?, party?, character?, at}`): a player seat opens in player mode; a party opens referee mode with that party in use; a character opens referee mode as that character, seen the way their player would see them (their sheet, followers and what they know on the map), which works for NPCs with no player seat, with a "Referee, as <name>" bar and an "All parties" link back; nothing more opens referee mode with every party. The Companion writes back its own pick when it switches.
