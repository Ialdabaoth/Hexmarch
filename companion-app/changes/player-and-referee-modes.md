Player mode and referee mode (Heph 2026-11-09). The campaign switcher (Settings › Campaign) lists every invitation on this device, Referee or Player, so the same campaign shows once for each invitation, and picking one sets the mode. Referee mode is the Companion as it was. Player mode shows your own character and their followers (no switching characters). Campaign › Your party lists the rest of your party from the shared state file, and the NPCs your character has met or heard of. The state is read-only. Creatures, referee maps, Art and Manage are hidden. Campaign › Maps opens the Atlas locked to your character's knowledge. Invitations are kept under `invitations` on the device (`src/lib/invitations.ts`). A player's invitation is a link, `?campaign=<link>&as=player&character=<id>`, copied from Settings › Invite a player or pasted into Add a campaign. Campaigns opened before this become referee invitations. Knowledge entries gain `creature` and `name` (CHARACTER_FORMAT.md "Knowledge"): met, or heard of with `"rumour": true`, as for hexes. Each creature in Campaign › Creatures has **The party met them** and **The party heard of them**.
